Where nature meets dining

Drishya means “view” in Nepali. Our restaurant is where dining and nature come together near Begnas Lake (Begnas Tal), surrounded by greenery, fresh air and the atmosphere of Pachbhaiya.

We invite you to slow down, relax and enjoy the moment.

  • Greenery

    Natural greenery and peaceful views all around.

  • Begnas Lake

    The refreshing atmosphere of the Begnas Lake surroundings.

  • Cool air and rain

    Feel the cool breeze and see the hills at their freshest on rainy days.

  • Peaceful dining

    Relax, enjoy your meal and take in the natural surroundings.
